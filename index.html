About Us

Our Story

The Wedding Blouse – by Kaaru gives our brides a truly meaningful and complete piece of bridal artistry, making her special day even more memorable.

We are dedicated to creating timeless bridal blouses that embody love, tradition, and artistry, making every bride’s special day unforgettable.

What We Do at The Wedding Blouse – by Kaaru

  • Personal Consultation – We sit with every bride to understand her story, style, and vision.
  • Design Detailing – Together, we create unique blouse designs with intricate elements and personal touches.
  • Perfect Stitch & Style – From cut to finish, every blouse is tailored to perfection.
  • Theme Coordination – Ensuring the blouse blends beautifully with the bridal theme and outfit.
  • Our Signature Touch – With our expert inputs, the design evolves into a flawless masterpiece.
